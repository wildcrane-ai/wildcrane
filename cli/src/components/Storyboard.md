# Storyboard

Show a sequence with `<Storyboard src=".assets/<card id>/<storyboard>.json" label="..." />` alone in a paragraph; `label` is optional. Inline and fenced examples remain text.

- **Frames**: a shot frame or slide preview may be anything an Asset shows, but not a storyboard or compare.
- **Data contract**: follow the producing agent's storyboard contract and validator for its shots or slides, keeping stable IDs and reachable preview files.
